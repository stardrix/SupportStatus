# Guide for site staff

With the admin window you show your colleagues whether you are open, phone only, closed or forwarded
to the central desk, and you manage your own opening hours.

## Open the admin window

1. Find the **Support status** window in the bottom right corner of the screen. If it is hidden, click the round **IT** icon next to the clock (it may be under **^**).
2. Press the green **Set my status** button at the top of the window.
3. Check that your location is selected under **Location**.

Cannot see **Set my status**? Ask your IT admins to start the app in admin mode on your PC.

## Set your status right now

Use it when you differ from your normal hours, e.g. when you are out on a job.

1. Optionally type a short message, e.g. "Out on a job".
2. Choose in **Valid until** how long it should last: **Midnight (rest of the day)** or a time, e.g. **10:00**.
3. Press **Open**, **Phone only** or **Closed**.
4. Your colleagues see it within 30 seconds.

- **Change the message afterwards:** type the new message and press **Save message** (or Enter).
- **The status lasts until the chosen time (or midnight).** After that your normal opening hours apply again by themselves.
- **Back to normal early:** press **Back to normal opening hours**.

## Forward your phone to the central desk

Always do this when you forward your phone, e.g. when you leave. Then the central desk knows the calls are coming to them.

1. Tick **The phone is forwarded to …** in the admin window.
2. Or quicker: right-click the IT icon next to the clock and choose **Forwarded to …**. This works for your location with the ★.
3. **When you are back:** clear the tick the same way.

The tick stays, also overnight and at weekends, until you clear it. Meanwhile everyone sees you as **FORWARDED** (blue).

## Change your opening hours

Do this when your regular hours change. The hours apply until you change them again.

1. Find **Opening hours** at the bottom of the admin window. There is one row per day, Monday to Sunday.
2. Tick **Walk-in** and/or **Phone** on the days you are open, and choose from and to.
3. Clear the ticks on the days you are closed.
4. If all weekdays are the same, fill in Monday and press **Copy Monday to Tue-Fri**.
5. Press **Save opening hours**. "Saved at …" appears at the bottom when it is saved.

All day is **00:00** to **24:00**.

## Everyday examples

| Situation | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| You are out on a job and can only answer the phone | Type "Out on a job", choose Valid until = 13:00, and press **Phone only**. |
| You are ill or on a course all day | Optionally type "Please call the service desk", and press **Closed**. |
| You are back early | Press **Back to normal opening hours**. |
| You open on an extra Saturday | Press **Open** that day. Or tick Saturday under opening hours if it is permanent. |
| Your regular hours change from next week | Change the schedule under **Opening hours** and press **Save opening hours**. |
| You leave and forward your phone | Right-click the IT icon next to the clock and choose **Forwarded to …**. Clear it again the next morning. |

## If something goes wrong

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "Could not save …" (status, opening hours or forwarding) | You do not have write access to the status folder. Contact your IT admins. |
| Your change does not show for colleagues | Wait up to 30 seconds. The window refreshes twice a minute. |
| You cannot find the window | Click the round **IT** icon next to the clock (under **^**). |
| A phone number, email or address is wrong | Contact your IT admins. They are maintained centrally. |

Remember: everyone who can see the status can read your messages.
